  • Designed a twin engine thrust vectoring module in SolidWorks from two copies of my 12 primary and 12 secondary petal variable area exhaust nozzle, mounted side by side on a shared bulkhead with a custom stand. Each nozzle sits in a printed two axis (pitch and yaw) cardan gimbal with plus or minus 15 degrees of travel, driven through servo horn arms and ball jointed pushrods, while the nozzle's own actuation ring changes exit area independently of the gimbal.
  • Iteratively redesigned the primary petal hinge and crank geometry to reduce mechanism packaging while preserving actuation kinematics, and verified in CAD a 3.85 mm actuator stroke with interference-free operation from 0° to 15.5° petal rotation.
  • Modeled the full assembly with all fasteners, mated the secondary petals to follow the actuation ring so each nozzle moves as one mechanism, and designed friction clamp stand in arms so the module can be posed by hand until servos are fitted.
  • Completed a production drawing with GD&T (ASME Y14.5 2018) for the nozzle housing, including a datum scheme, position tolerances on the hole patterns, and detail views of the hinge features. Drawings for the remaining nozzle parts are in progress.
  • Status (in progress, Sep. 2026 to present): 3D printing the full assembly to test fit, kinematics, and gimbal range.

  • Selected a 44°C-melt paraffin wax over a tested 54.67°C alternative through empirical melt testing, sized it via first-principles heat-transfer calculations, and validated melt behavior with an ANSYS transient thermal simulation.
